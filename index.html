Our Story

Four decades of pure vegetarian cooking

Since 1986, Porwal Bhojnalya has been serving the Sikandra neighbourhood near Kargil Petrol Pump with simple, wholesome, 100% vegetarian meals — the same recipes, the same care, three generations on.

  • No onion, no garlic, no non-vegetarian ingredient ever enters our kitchen.
  • Dal simmered slow, breads made fresh to order on the tawa.
  • Half & full portions on most dishes, so nothing goes to waste.
  • Dine-in, takeaway, and free delivery within 1 km.

🟢 Our Promise

Every single item on our menu is marked with the pure vegetarian mark. There is no non-vegetarian item, ever, on our premises.
